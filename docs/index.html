mcacc-collars

I make collars, leashes & "adopt me" leash sleeves for homeless dogs. I created this site to share how-to resources for others who may want to do this too. The "value proposition" (making ten for the cost of buying one at retail to donate) can be a compelling reason. The following describes my material costs (mid-2026):

Leash sleeves
